About Me

I'm Mollie, a CS graduate from SMU's Bobby B. Lyle School of Engineering, where I studied in the AI and Machine Learning track. I build full-stack systems, research automation tools, and database-driven applications, and I care deeply about writing code that actually works in production.

My background spans software internships at Nokia (SBC R&D), academic research at SMU's Matthews Research Group automating molecular excitation pattern recognition, and ML projects ranging from RAG pipelines to medical image analysis.

I also come from marketing. I ran campaigns for WISE and led HackSMU VII's marketing. I think that background makes me a sharper engineer: I build with the end user in mind and I know how to communicate what I ship. Currently seeking a full-time software engineering role in New York City.
